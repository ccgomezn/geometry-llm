# arXiv endorsement request

**How endorsement works (note for you, not part of the email).** arXiv asks first-time submitters to some
categories, including cs.LG and cs.CL, to be endorsed by someone who has already published enough in that category.
When you start a submission, arXiv shows whether you need an endorsement, and gives you an endorsement code and a link
to forward. The endorser opens the link, enters the code, and that's it. They only vouch that this is a genuine
research paper in the right area; they don't review it or co-sign it. Any arXiv page lists, under "Which authors of
this paper are endorsers?", who can endorse. Good people to ask are authors of the closest related work (e.g. papers
cited in the draft) or researchers you know. Ask one person at a time, and keep it short.

Before sending: replace everything in [brackets], attach the PDF (`paper/arxiv/main.pdf`) or a link to it, and
include the endorsement code arXiv gave you.
