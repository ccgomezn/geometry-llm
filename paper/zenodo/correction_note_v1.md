> Correction (November 2026). Two claims in this version do not hold: that erasing the map "costs no more accuracy
> than erasing random directions", and that "after erasure the probe's R² is at most 0.09" (Section 3.2). Both rest on
> a one-shot erasure that does not hold in the running model, and on a random control that also erased the first
> token. With a verified erasure and a fair control, the erased directions do change the answers, mainly by feeding a
> one-sentence shortcut. A corrected and extended paper is in preparation. The corrected results, preregistrations and
> code are at https://github.com/ccgomezn/geometry-llm/tree/outvoted-map-v1
